Sudoku

Tap a square, then a number. Keyboard: 1–9 to enter, arrows to move, P to switch pen and pencil, Backspace to erase. Correct numbers earn points (more on harder levels), mistakes cost 5, and finishing earns a bonus.

Chess Puzzles

Your rating 1200 Solved 0Streak 0

Tap a piece, then tap where it should go. Your rating starts at 1200 and moves up or down after each puzzle, the same way online chess ratings work. Using a hint means no rating change; a wrong move or showing the solution counts as a miss. Puzzles come from the Lichess open database (public domain).

What it does

Badger Vocab helps you practice vocabulary in 48 languages with a matching game, flashcards and word lists. In Free Learn you can make your own decks about any subject, with pictures. Study Break adds Sudoku and chess puzzles for a breather. Rate each card as “don’t know,” “semi-know” or “know,” and the ones you find hardest come back more often.

Your privacy

Your words and progress are saved only on this device. There are no accounts, and nothing you type is uploaded. The one exception is read-aloud with the Google voice: when you’re online, the word being spoken is sent to Google Translate to get the audio. Choose “Device voice only” in a language’s settings to turn that off.
